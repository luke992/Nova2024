Nova 2024 is a Generative AI themed Hackathon hosted by ScottyLabs at Carnegie Mellon University. 
Our team, consisting of Luke Hann, Alistair Cheong, Nathan Chen, and Hani Murtaza worked together over the course of 7 hours to create ATLAS.
ATLAS is a Generative AI Map Analysis Tool that uses ChatGPT and Open Street Map.
Using the world map provided by Open Street Map, users of ATLAS can query our AI about locations and parts of the world. ChatGPT will then take those queries, and using the databases provided by Open Street Map,
give accurate pointers that relate to the user's query. 

Example of the interface:
For every query the user gives, a seperate layer on top of the base map is created, allowing for easy switching between different queries without obscuring any of them.

ATLAS was created with the intention of being a better alternative to current Map Tools, such as Google or Apple Maps. 
ATLAS utilizes Open Street Map, a free volunteer created and maintained map of the world, with focus on heavily underrepresented areas, such as third world countries.
There is no copyright issues with Open Street Map and users are free to modify and innovate as they please, which allowed ATLAS to exist in the first place. 
We created ATLAS to be quick and easy tool for everyone, easily finding specific information for whatever location they need.
We also created ATLAS with the intention of being used by First Responders who quickly need to survey and analyze areas in order to help anyone in case of disaster or emergency.
The speed and precision of ATLAS, coupled with the detailed mapping of Open Street Map, allows First Responders to spend little time analysing while still getting the same if not more information about the land,
which allows them to spend more time saving lives.
